Privacy policy

This policy covers what the site collects when you write to the studio or join the notes.

Details you give us

The demo form asks for your name, work email, studio, the kind of work, and a production note. The newsletter asks for a work email.

Why we keep them

We use a demo request to reply about a studio pilot. We use a newsletter address to send the notes you asked for.

Tools on the page

The demo form uses Google reCAPTCHA to check that a person sent it. The site chat is provided by Tawk.to. Those tools receive what they need to run the check or the conversation.

Asking for a change

You can ask us to delete a note or a newsletter address through the contact form on the site.
